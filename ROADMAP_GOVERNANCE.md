# Roadmap governance

DJConnect owns product priority. TDE governance owns the integrity of TDE's
public contracts, but prioritises only direct, demonstrated DJConnect pipeline
value during Generation 2.

An active backlog item documents its concrete DJConnect value, intended
consumer, acceptance evidence, and non-goals. It must also satisfy the five
investment questions in [PLATFORM_STRATEGY.md](PLATFORM_STRATEGY.md). Items
that do not qualify remain post-1.0 options rather than implicit commitments.

Capability evolution continues to require a documented scope, canonical-model
mapping, qualification impact, evidence compatibility statement, and release
plan. Schema evolution remains versioned, additive where possible, and
fail-closed for incompatible versions. This governance increment changes none
of those contracts.
