# Roadmap governance

The TDE roadmap is owned by the TDE product. Prioritization considers reusable product value, contract integrity, security, maintainability, and evidence quality—not a single consumer's internal structure.

Capability evolution requires a documented scope, adapter independence, canonical-model mapping, qualification impact, evidence compatibility statement, and release plan. Schema evolution is versioned, additive where possible, and fail-closed for unknown incompatible versions.
