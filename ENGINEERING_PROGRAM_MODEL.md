# Engineering program model

| Program | Purpose | Ownership | Promotion rules | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Core Runtime | Deliver supported TDE contracts and capabilities. | TDE product engineering. | Must satisfy architecture, contract, evidence, qualification, and validation requirements. | Highest for committed Generation 1 outcomes. |
| Platform Evolution | Improve architecture, schemas, governance, and long-term platform capability. | TDE architecture governance. | Requires a focused decision and compatibility statement where public contracts change. | Strategic. |
| Innovation Lab | Research uncertain ideas without production commitments. | Designated research ownership under TDE governance. | Requires explicit approval before promotion to another program. | Exploratory. |

Programs may inform each other but do not bypass their promotion rules. Innovation research does not deliver production functionality.
