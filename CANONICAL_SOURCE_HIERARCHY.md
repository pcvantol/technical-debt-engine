# Canonical source hierarchy

When sources conflict, the higher source prevails. A lower source must be corrected through a focused increment; it cannot redefine a higher source by implication.

1. [Platform Vision](PLATFORM_VISION.md)
2. [Platform Strategy](PLATFORM_STRATEGY.md)
3. [Engineering Method](ENGINEERING_METHOD.md)
4. [Architecture Decisions](architecture/adr)
5. [Product Architecture](PRODUCT_ARCHITECTURE.md)
6. [Capability Model](CAPABILITY_MODEL.md)
7. [Product Roadmap](PRODUCT_ROADMAP.md)
8. [Product Backlog](PRODUCT_BACKLOG.md)
9. [Prompt Index](PROMPT_INDEX.md)
10. [Repository Status](REPOSITORY_STATUS.md)
11. [Management Summary](MANAGEMENT_SUMMARY.md)
12. Implementation
13. Generated Evidence

The hierarchy applies to humans, AI agents, implementation, and generated output. Generated evidence may report facts but cannot alter a policy or architecture decision. A conflict involving the Engineering Method requires a dedicated Engineering Governance prompt.
