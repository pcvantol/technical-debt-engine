# ADR-0001: AI-Native Engineering Method

## Status

Accepted

## Context

TDE requires repeatable collaboration between human engineers and AI agents without surrendering product governance.

## Decision

Adopt the canonical AI-Native Engineering Method in [ENGINEERING_METHOD.md](../../ENGINEERING_METHOD.md), with BOOTSTRAP as the mandatory session entry point.

## Consequences

Sessions follow canonical sources, focused prompts, validation, and reviewable pull requests. Human authority remains required for merge and release.

## Alternatives

Ad hoc AI use and undocumented local workflows were rejected because they reduce traceability and architectural consistency.
