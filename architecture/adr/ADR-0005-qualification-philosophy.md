# ADR-0005: Qualification Philosophy

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Measurements alone do not define acceptable engineering risk.

## Decision

Qualification applies explicit, deterministic, fail-closed policy to compatible evidence and baselines.

## Consequences

Missing, malformed, or incompatible inputs cannot silently pass; decisions remain explainable through policy and evidence.

## Alternatives

Implicit thresholds and permissive handling of unknown evidence were rejected because they create false assurance.
