# Innovation lab

The Innovation Lab researches hypotheses, prototypes, and uncertain engineering approaches. It does not deliver production functionality, establish public contracts, receive release authority, or bypass normal qualification.

Generation 2 research themes are AI recommendations, automatic refactoring suggestions, predictive trends, engineering forecasting, architecture drift prediction, and technical-debt simulation.

Promotion requires explicit human approval, a defined target program, architecture and contract review where applicable, evidence of value and risk, and a focused canonical prompt. Unpromoted work remains research and must be clearly labelled as such.
