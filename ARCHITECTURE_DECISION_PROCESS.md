# Architecture decision process

TDE architecture evolves through concise, durable Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) in [architecture/adr](architecture/adr). Every ADR states status, context, decision, consequences, and alternatives; it may also state ownership and compatibility impact.

An ADR is required when a change alters a public contract, capability boundary, canonical model, qualification semantics, evidence schema, consumer integration model, or engineering constitution. Decisions are owned by TDE product governance, reviewed proportionately to risk, and remain traceable through releases. Consumers may propose changes but do not own the architecture.
