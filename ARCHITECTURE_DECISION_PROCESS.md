# Architecture decision process

TDE architecture evolves through concise, durable decisions that state context, decision, alternatives, consequences, owner, and compatibility impact.

An architecture decision is required when a change alters a public contract, capability boundary, canonical model, qualification semantics, evidence schema, or consumer integration model. Decisions are owned by TDE product governance, reviewed proportionately to risk, and remain traceable through releases. Consumers may propose changes but do not own the architecture.
